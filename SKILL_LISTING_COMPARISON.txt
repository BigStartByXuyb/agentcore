═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
WHY THIS ORDER?
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. SPEED: User message doesn't wait for skill discovery to complete
2. PREFETCH: Skills are being discovered while Claude reads and responds
3. CONTEXT: Skills are available for follow-up tool calls in next iteration
4. ASYNC: getAttachmentMessages uses async generators for streaming yield
